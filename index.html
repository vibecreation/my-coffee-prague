OUR STORY

A quiet garden, right in the middle of the city

Just a few minutes from the Powder Tower, MY COFFEE is a small family-run café built around one simple idea: real coffee and desserts made in their own bakery, served in a genuinely peaceful inner garden. Away from Prague's tourist crush, it's the kind of place regulars protect as their own quiet secret — cozy, green, and unmistakably homemade.
